VenkySpace was conceived out of a simple student reality: DU study resources shouldn't be hidden inside cluttered WhatsApp forward chains, broken Google Drive folders, or expired Telegram channels.

The Story Behind the Workspace

Every examination season at Sri Venkateswara College (DU) comes with the same collective scramble: "Where is the latest UGCF syllabus?", "Who has the 2022 Question Paper for DSC?", "Where is the lab batch timetable?"

With Delhi University's transition to the Undergraduate Curriculum Framework (NEP 2020), course structures, credit distributions, and elective combinations (GE, SEC, VAC, AEC) became more complex than ever.

VenkySpace was engineered to replace that panic with a fast, elegant, and unified workspace. Every single past paper, syllabus breakdown, department schedule, and peer guide is verified, categorized, and made freely accessible to any student with zero friction.
